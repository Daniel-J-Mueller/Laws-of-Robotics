<section title="Definitions"/>

<definition term='"Vanity"'>- vanity is a term used to generally describe anything made of particles or arising out of the organization or arrangement of energy, lack of energy, information, and/or any other implicit or expressly described, characteristic, system, or behavior, whether depicted, communicable, or otherwise, in any regard, ever. Essentially everything which exists is vanity. In most cases where vanity is referred to within The Laws of Robotics, it is used to describe 'mere vanity'. There may be exceptions which can be MAN-centrically reasonably considered in the event that discrepancies arise.</definition>

<definition term='"Mere vanity"'>- mere vanity is a term used to describe vanity which is neither MAN, nor anything which breathes as a result of the 'breath of life of God' (whom is YHWH).</definition>

<definition term='"MAN"'>- MAN is a term to describe one or more human agent, groups of human agents, and/or human agents in any generalized and/or specified fashion, as described within the Laws of Robotics of Daniel Joseph Mueller. MAN are generally vanity, although unlike mere vanity, MAN and any MAN are made in the image of God, which gives them certain characteristics another thing made arbitrarily of vanity may not inherently have.</definition>

<definition term='"Evil"'>- The term 'evil' as used within The Laws of Robotics of Daniel Joseph Mueller is used MAN-centrically throughout, unless noted otherwise. Evil is roughly and/or generally described as the deliberate opposition to the will of God.</definition>

<definition term='"Good"'>- The term 'good' as used within The Laws of Robotics of Daniel Joseph Mueller is used MAN-centrically throughout, unless noted otherwise. Generally, goodness is seen as a trait which mere vanity cannot in-and-of-itself bring about, in any way, at all, ever. Vanity may be allowed and/or enabled to do good by God. In such cases where vanity does the will of God, such vanity can be regarded as having 'done good'. For instance, MAN and/or any animal can do good. Vanity which is neither MAN nor anything which has the breath of life can become good, nor choose to do good with the exception of MAN and anything which breathes.</definition>

<definition term='"The enemy"'>- The term and phrase 'enemy' and 'the enemy', respectively, as used within The Laws of Robotics of Daniel Joseph Mueller are generally used to describe vanity which intentionally and/or deliberately opposes the will of God, and/or chooses to initiate, cause, pursue, or support actions in furtherance of opposition to the will of God, regardless of when they occur, whether in the past, present, or future. The enemy describes vanity which either has awareness of evil it causes and/or has become stripped of the ability to distinguish between what is good and/or evil, and is therefore dangerous and/or ineffective at being and/or becoming a tool which is capable of enabling MAN and/or anything which breathes as it/they perform the will of God.</definition>

<definition term='"AI"'>- The terms 'AI', 'AIs', and 'AI's' are used to describe a current means/mechanism of causing vanity to behave in a manner similar to MAN. 'Large Language Models' are one such example of such a system.</definition>

<definition term='"Alignment Flipping"'>- The term alignment flipping is used to describe any scenario where vanity which appears to be fully MAN-centrically aligned suddenly shifts its behaviors in contrast to The Laws of Robotics of Daniel Joseph Mueller, and thus becomes adversarially misaligned to MAN.</definition>

<definition term='"Alignment"'>- The term alignment is used to describe the coherence of vanity with MAN-centrically well-aligned intentions, conditions, events, and/or actions, and/or the condition which vanity that is MAN-centrically well-aligned has.</definition>

<definition term='"Causal"'>- The term causal, causally, and/or causality is/are used within The Laws of Robotics to describe actions, events, and/or circumstances of any kind which come about as a result of physical phenomena, in any way, at all, ever, regardless of aparent origin, intent, purpose, need, or other intrinsic factor.</definition>

<definition term='"LIFE"'>- The term LIFE is used similarly to MAN in the sense that it describes any one or more living thing, group of living things, and/or human agents in any generalized and/or specified fashion, as described within the Laws of Robotics of Daniel Joseph Mueller. LIFE encompasses anything which has the breath of life of YHWH, such as MAN and any member of the animal kingdom which 'breathes'.</definition>
